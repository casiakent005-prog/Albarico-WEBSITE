Cebu Province

At the heart of the Philippines lies Cebu Province, a destination that blends history, nature, culture, and modern charm into a single unforgettable experience. Cebu is not just one city but a thriving province of 51 towns and cities, each contributing to its reputation as the country’s most compelling tourism hub.

People, commerce, culture, experience — the four winds of the province.

01 — The People

From galleon decks to global stage

Cebu has been a trading floor for a thousand years. Today, its furniture, guitars, and design houses move from the workshops of Mandaue to showrooms in Milan and Tokyo — a province exporting taste.

“Built by the salt-air humidity of the islands.”

02 — Commerce

A smile shaped by four centuries

Cebuanos are the descendants of Rajah Humabon's court and Lapu-Lapu's defiance. Four centuries of trade, faith, and typhoons have produced a people who are genuinely warm, genuinely funny, and quietly impossible to rush.

“Bisaya ko, taga-Sugbo.”

03 — Culture

The dance is the prayer

Sinulog is not a parade. It is a city-wide act of devotion — bodies in two-step, drums in 4/4, candles passing through smoke. The ritual predates the cathedral and outlives every empire that staged it.

“The dance is older than the saint.”

04 — Experience

The flavor profile of the south

The lechon of Talisay and Carcar. The tablea sikwate of Argao. The sun-dried mangoes of Mactan, exported to 50 countries and still best eaten fresh off a rooftop. Cebuano food is a celebration of where it came from.

“A taste that lingers long after the sun sets.”

Best of Cebu

A curated collection of Cebu's most iconic destinations and cultural treasures — from world-class natural wonders to time-honored traditions. From the turquoise waters of Bantayan in the north to the majestic whale sharks of Oslob in the south, Cebu Province is a story of many places woven into one destination. It is a living showcase of how the Philippines’ past, present, and future come together — making Cebu not only a gateway but also a journey in itself.

Discover Cebu

The Cebu Map serves as an interactive visual guide that presents the province's key destinations and tourism offerings in an organized and accessible format. As visitors explore and select each area on the map, they are guided to discover the diverse experiences that Cebu has to offer — from its cultural and heritage sites to its natural attractions, and adventure destinations.

It provides a comprehensive overview that allows visitors to navigate the province with ease, offering a clearer appreciation of Cebu's distinct tourism landscape across its districts.
