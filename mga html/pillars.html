CEBU'S DISTINCT PILLARS

Cebu's tourism direction is guided by its distinct pillars that highlight the province's culture, heritage, nature, and livelihood experiences. These pillars serve as the foundation for showcasing Cebu as a complete and diverse destination.

These are further presented through the four distinct brochures, each representing a curated selection of destinations and experiences across different areas of the province. Together, they provide visitors with a clear and organized guide to exploring Cebu's unique attractions, stories, and local experiences from north to south.

Adventure

Choose your own adventure across Cebu — canyoneering, mountaineering, diving, island hopping, and more. From the highlands of Osmeña Peak to the canyons of Kawasan, Cebu is built for the bold.

Dive

Cebu is one of the Philippines' premier dive destinations — from the thresher sharks of Malapascua to the sardine run of Moalboal and the vibrant reefs of Badian — diving in every direction.

Culture and Heritage

Cebu is the birthplace of Philippine Christianity and one of the country's most storied provinces. From centuries-old churches and Spanish forts to ancestral houses and heritage streets, history lives in every corner.

Explore

Navigate Cebu's 51 municipalities and cities with this comprehensive guide — destinations, experiences, and tourism offices mapped across the province from north to south.
