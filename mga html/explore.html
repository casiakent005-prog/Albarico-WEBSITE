EXPLORE CEBU

Cebu Province is a thriving mosaic of 51 distinct towns and cities, each carving out its own unique footprint between a rugged mountain spine and more than 500 kilometers of crystal-clear coastline. From the heritage-rich streets of our oldest colonial settlements to the untouched coral reefs of our northern and southern island sanctuaries, this interactive visual guide is designed to help you navigate the province with absolute ease. Select any municipality or city on the lists below to unlock its complete, dedicated travel profile — including top natural and historical attractions, authentic local flavors, optimal seasonal windows to visit, and direct contact details for local tourism offices. Your journey into the heart of Sugbo begins here.

51 LGUs

01

Alcantara

Alcantara is a small coastal municipality located on the southwestern side of Cebu Province. Known as the "Home of the Bahandi Festival," it is blessed with rich marine resources, fertile agricultural lands, and a peaceful rural atmosphere. The municipality was formerly known as Kugtong, named after a legendary giant fish said to inhabit a spring in the town center before it was renamed by the Spaniards after a town in Spain.

02

Alcoy

Alcoy is a charming coastal municipality in southeastern Cebu known for its calm beaches, diving spots, and peaceful rural atmosphere. The town is home to Tingko Beach, one of South Cebu's most beloved white-sand beaches, and a thriving marine ecosystem ideal for snorkeling and scuba diving. Renowned for its rolling hills, pristine beaches, and lush mountains, Alcoy is the habitat of the endemic Black Shama (Copsychus cebuensis), a rare songbird, and hosts significant dolomite deposits, supporting its emerging eco-tourism economy focused on sustainable agriculture, fishing, and natural attractions.

Explore
03

Alegria

Alegria, Cebu is a quiet coastal municipality in Cebu famous for its dramatic limestone cliffs, lush mountains, and turquoise waters. Known as an eco-tourism and adventure hub, it offers a relaxed, rural vibe—making it a premier destination for canyoneering and waterfall trekking away from the commercialized areas.

Explore
04

Aloguinsan

Aloguinsan is a thriving municipality located on the western coast of Cebu Province. Renowned for its commitment to sustainable tourism, Aloguinsan is home to rich natural landscapes, mangrove ecosystems, and community-based ecotourism initiatives that showcase the municipality's dedication to environmental conservation and local development.

Explore
05

Argao

Argao is a vibrant, first-class coastal town in southeastern Cebu established as a Spanish pueblo in 1735. It is highly celebrated for its rich culinary and craft heritage, particularly its four signature assets: Torta (traditional sponge cake baked with palm wine), Tuba (coconut wine), Tablea (cacao tablets), and Hablon (handwoven textile).

Explore
06

Asturias

Asturias is a vibrant municipality located on the northwestern coast of Cebu Province. Known for its agricultural abundance, scenic mountain landscapes, and growing ecotourism destinations, Asturias offers visitors a blend of natural beauty, rural charm, and cultural heritage.

Explore
07

Badian

Badian is a first-class municipality in southern Cebu, Philippines, famous for its natural wonders, waterfalls, and adventure tourism. It is best known for Kawasan Falls and for activities like canyoneering, trekking, and river adventures. Badian is a perfect destination for nature lovers and adventure seekers.

Explore
08

Balamban

Balamban is a first-class municipality located on the western coast of Cebu Province. Known as the "Shipbuilding Capital of the Philippines," it is home to world-class shipbuilding facilities, scenic mountain landscapes, and emerging ecotourism destinations. Balamban offers a unique blend of industrial progress, natural beauty, and cultural heritage.

Explore
09

Bantayan

Bantayan is a first-class island municipality located in the northernmost part of Cebu Province. Renowned for its powdery white-sand beaches, crystal-clear waters, and laid-back island atmosphere, Bantayan has become one of Cebu's top tourist destinations. The municipality combines natural beauty, rich history, and vibrant local traditions, making it a favorite getaway for both local and international visitors.

Explore
10

Barili

Barili is a historic coastal municipality located on the southwestern coast of Cebu Province. Known for its rich cultural heritage, scenic landscapes, agricultural abundance, and thriving fishing industry, Barili offers a unique blend of history, nature, and local traditions.

Explore
11

Bogo City

Bogo City is a vibrant component city located in the northern part of Cebu Province. Serving as the commercial, educational, and transportation hub of northern Cebu, Bogo is known for its rich history, thriving economy, and strategic location that connects mainland Cebu to the northern islands.

Explore
12

Boljoon

Boljoon is a first-class municipality in southern Cebu, Philippines, known for its rich historical heritage and coastal charm. It is often called the "Heritage Town of Southern Cebu" due to its well-preserved Spanish-era architecture, centuries-old churches, and cobblestone streets. Boljoon also offers stunning beaches, diving spots, and cultural attractions.

Explore
13

Borbon

Borbon is a peaceful coastal municipality located in the northeastern part of Cebu Province. Known for its scenic coastline, rich agricultural lands, and warm community spirit, Borbon offers a blend of natural beauty, rural charm, and cultural heritage that reflects the traditional Cebuano way of life.

Explore
14

Carcar City

Carcar City is a historic component city located in the southern part of Cebu Province. Renowned as one of Cebu's heritage destinations, Carcar is famous for its well-preserved Spanish and American-era architecture, rich cultural traditions, vibrant arts scene, and distinctive local delicacies. The city seamlessly blends history, culture, and commerce, making it a must-visit destination in southern Cebu.

Explore
15

Carmen

Carmen is a first-class municipality in northern Cebu, Philippines, best known as the home of Cebu Safari and Adventure Park, the largest safari park in the Philippines. Blending rural charm with a major tourism draw, Carmen features rolling hills, agricultural lands, and emerging eco-tourism destinations.

Explore
16

Catmon

Catmon is a charming coastal municipality located in the northern part of Cebu Province. Known for its scenic coastline, natural springs, waterfalls, and rich agricultural resources, Catmon offers a peaceful countryside experience complemented by its vibrant local culture and heritage.

Explore
17

City of Naga

City of Naga is a thriving component city located in the southern part of Cebu Province. Known as the "Industrial Hub of Southern Cebu," Naga is recognized for its rapid economic growth, industrial developments, rich cultural heritage, and emerging tourism destinations. The city seamlessly combines progress and tradition, making it an important center for business, industry, and culture.

Explore
18

Compostela

Compostela is a progressive coastal municipality located in the northeastern part of Cebu Province. Known for its rich agricultural resources, growing commercial sector, and scenic coastal landscapes, Compostela offers a harmonious blend of rural charm, cultural heritage, and modern development.

Explore
19

Consolacion

A rapidly urbanizing first-class municipality in northern Metro Cebu, Consolacion acts as a gateway to the north. It balances modern commercial shopping malls with deep-rooted agricultural traditions.

Explore
20

Cordova

Cordova is a coastal municipality located on Mactan Island in Cebu Province. Known for its rich marine resources, thriving fishing communities, and picturesque coastal landscapes, Cordova offers a unique blend of island charm, cultural heritage, and modern tourism developments. It serves as the gateway to several beautiful islets and marine sanctuaries in the province.

Explore
21

Daanbantayan

Daanbantayan is a first-class coastal municipality located at the northernmost tip of mainland Cebu. Known as the gateway to the world-famous Malapascua Island, the municipality boasts pristine beaches, rich marine biodiversity, historic landmarks, and a vibrant fishing industry. Its unique blend of natural beauty and cultural heritage makes it one of northern Cebu's premier destinations.

Explore
22

Dalaguete

Dalaguete is a first-class municipality located on the southeastern coast of Cebu Province. Known as the "Vegetable Basket of Cebu," it is renowned for its fertile highlands, thriving agricultural industry, scenic mountain landscapes, and rich cultural heritage. The municipality is also a popular destination for outdoor enthusiasts, serving as the gateway to some of Cebu's most iconic hiking and nature attractions.

Explore
23

Danao City

Danao City is a thriving component city located in the northern part of Cebu Province. Known for its rich history, vibrant economy, and growing tourism industry, Danao is recognized as a center of commerce, manufacturing, and adventure tourism in northern Cebu. The city offers a unique blend of cultural heritage, natural attractions, and modern development.

Explore
24

Dumanjug

Dumanjug is a first-class coastal municipality located on the southwestern coast of Cebu Province. Known for its rich history, agricultural abundance, scenic coastline, and strong cultural traditions, Dumanjug serves as an important economic and transportation hub in southwestern Cebu. The municipality offers a blend of natural attractions, heritage sites, and warm Cebuano hospitality.

Explore
25

Ginatilan

Ginatilan is a quiet, pristine coastal gem. It is celebrated for its dramatic waterfalls, ancestral stone structures, and as the proud hometown of the second Filipino saint, San Pedro Calungsod.

Explore
26

Liloan

Liloan is a thriving, upscale coastal town in northeastern Cebu. Known as the "The Light of the North," it is highly famous for its historic lighthouses, beautiful beach resorts, and its iconic local delicacy, Masi (sweet peanut-filled sticky rice balls).

Explore
27

Madridejos

Situated at the northernmost tip of Bantayan Island facing the rich waters of the Visayan Sea, Madridejos is the island's primary commercial fishing hub. Unlike its white-sand neighbor Santa Fe, Madridejos features a rugged coastline and a bustling maritime economy driven by massive commercial fishing fleets, poultry farming, and dried fish production.

Explore
28

Malabuyoc

Nestled on the southwestern coast of Cebu facing the Tañon Strait, Malabuyoc is a deeply peaceful town boasting a remarkably diverse topography. From its narrow coastal plains, the land sharply rises into a rugged, mountainous interior that shelters geothermal hot springs, multi-tiered waterfalls, and dense, untouched tropical rainforests.

Explore
29

Mandaue City

Positioned in the central-eastern coastal region, Mandaue is a highly urbanized, first-class city recognized as the "Industrial Capital of Southern Philippines." It serves as a vital economic engine, hosting massive manufacturing plants, breweries, and a globally renowned furniture export industry, all while retaining deep historical roots.

Explore
30

Medellin

Famously dubbed the "Sugar Bowl of Cebu," Medellin is a massive agro-industrial town in the north dominated by sprawling sugarcane plantations. In recent years, it has successfully diversified its economy by developing eco-tourism attractions, cliff-diving spots, and adventure parks.

Explore
31

Minglanilla

Known as the "Sugat Capital of the South," Minglanilla is a rapidly urbanizing, first-class municipality south of Cebu City. It has transitioned from a quiet agricultural town into a bustling residential, commercial, and industrial center, though it remains most famous for its deeply rooted religious traditions.

Explore
32

Moalboal

Located on the southwestern coast, Moalboal is a world-class destination globally recognized for its vibrant coral reefs and the magnificent, year-round sardine run. It has evolved from a quiet fishing village into a bustling, international eco-tourism hub filled with dive resorts, freediving schools, and coastal nightlife.

Explore
33

Oslob

Oslob is a first-class municipality in southern Cebu, Philippines, widely known as a prime eco-tourism destination. It is famous for whale shark watching, beautiful beaches, waterfalls, and rich marine biodiversity. Oslob balances tourism with local fishing and agriculture, making it a vibrant community for both visitors and residents.

Explore
34

Pilar

Pilar is a peaceful island municipality located on Ponson Island in the Camotes Islands, Cebu Province. Known for its pristine beaches, crystal-clear waters, and laid-back island atmosphere, Pilar offers a tranquil escape where visitors can experience natural beauty, rich marine resources, and warm Cebuano hospitality.

Explore
35

Pinamungajan

Located on the mid-western coast of Cebu, Pinamungajan is a thriving agricultural and aquacultural town. It features extensive milkfish (bangus) ponds, rich farmlands, and a beautifully rugged coastline that boasts offshore sandbars and hidden mountain wave pools, making it a rising eco-tourism star.

Explore
36

Poro

Poro is the central commercial, political, and historical heart of the Camotes Islands. Geographically distinct from the flatter islands, Poro features a rugged, highly elevated topography with deep limestone caves, dense forests, and towering rocky coastal formations.

Explore
37

Ronda

Ronda is a quiet, unassuming southwestern coastal town that boasts unspoiled marine sanctuaries, hilly inland terrains, and a deeply peaceful provincial lifestyle. It remains largely untouched by heavy commercialization, making it a perfect retreat for those seeking authentic, slow-paced Cebuano provincial life.

Explore
38

Samboan

Samboan is a ruggedly beautiful southern municipality renowned as the "Waterfall Capital of Cebu." Characterized by dense, mountainous jungles that drop steeply into the sea, it offers spectacular, hidden river cascades alongside beautifully preserved, centuries-old Spanish colonial structures.

Explore
39

San Fernando

Positioned along the eastern coast south of Cebu City, San Fernando bridges the gap between urban industrialization and rural mountain tranquility. It is a major economic contributor due to its massive cement manufacturing plants, but its western mountainous interior hides pristine, lush resorts and deep, unexplored cave systems.

Explore
40

San Francisco

Located on Pacijan Island, San Francisco is the commercial and tourism capital of the Camotes group. It boasts the region's most extensive tourism infrastructure, driven by its massive, powdery white-sand shorelines and Lake Danao, the largest natural lake in the entire province of Cebu.

Explore
41

San Remigio

Boasting the longest continuous shoreline of any municipality in Cebu, San Remigio is the premier beach destination of the northwest coast. It serves as a vital maritime gateway with its Hagnaya Port connecting mainland Cebu to Bantayan Island, while its pristine waters hide some of the region's best dive sites and marine sanctuaries.

Explore
42

Santa Fe

As the principal gateway to Bantayan Island, Santa Fe is a globally recognized beach destination. Unlike the rocky coastlines of the mainland, Santa Fe is entirely endowed with flat, powdery white-sand beaches, crystal-clear shallow waters, and a vibrant, laid-back international expatriate and tourist community.

Explore
43

Santander

Located at the absolute southernmost tip of mainland Cebu, Santander is a critical maritime gateway. Its deep coastal waters and strategic location make it the primary ferry connection point to the neighboring islands of Negros and Siquijor. It features dramatic ocean views where the Tañon Strait meets the Bohol Sea.

Explore
44

Sibonga

Sibonga is a first-class municipality in southern Cebu, Philippines, known for its serene coastal areas, rolling hills, and historical landmarks. It is a peaceful town that balances agriculture, fishing, and emerging tourism, making it an ideal destination for travelers seeking nature, heritage, and cultural experiences.

Explore
45

Sogod

Located in northern Cebu, Sogod is a tranquil municipality characterized by its winding, picturesque coastal highway that hugs steep cliffs overlooking the Camotes Sea. It is an agricultural town that relies on farming and fishing, offering visitors a genuinely rustic, uncommercialized provincial charm alongside its hidden natural cold springs.

Explore
46

Tabogon

Located on the northeastern coast of mainland Cebu, Tabogon is defined by its dramatic, towering limestone cliffs that drop sharply into the sea. It remains a tranquil, off-the-beaten-path destination that escapes massive tourist crowds, offering hidden cold springs and quiet coves surrounded by highly agricultural terrain.

Explore
47

Tabuelan

Tucked away on the northwestern coast, Tabuelan is a serene, rapidly rising tourism municipality known for its powdery white beaches, winding, unpolluted rivers, and a highly relaxed beach-camping culture. It offers a quiet alternative to the more commercialized northern beaches.

Explore
48

Talisay City

Positioned immediately south of Cebu City, Talisay City serves as a major residential and commercial hub. It holds immense historical significance for its role in World War II and is celebrated province-wide as the undisputed lechon (roasted pig) capital of Cebu, boasting a thriving local culinary scene.

Explore
49

Toledo City

Known as the "Copper City with a Heart," Toledo is a massive industrial and mining hub on the western coast of Cebu. Beyond its heavy industries and bustling port (which connects to San Carlos City, Negros), its vast mountainous interior hides incredible man-made lakes, European-style gardens, and breathtaking landscapes shaped by decades of mining.

Explore
50

Tuburan

Tuburan is the largest municipality in Cebu in terms of land area. Located on the northwestern coast, it is an eco-tourism powerhouse famous for its abundant crystal-clear cold springs, rugged marble canyons, and its rapidly emerging status as the highland coffee capital of the province.

Explore
51

Tudela

Tudela is a municipality in Camotes Islands, Cebu, Philippines, known for its peaceful beaches, fishing communities, and scenic inland landscapes. It is an ideal destination for travelers seeking quiet island life and natural beauty, away from crowded tourist spots.
